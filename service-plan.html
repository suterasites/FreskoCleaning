Why a plan

Clean looks good. Maintained lasts longer.

Sparkling

Gutters, solar panels, windows and walls cleaned on a schedule, so your home looks its best every season, not just after a one-off clean.

Longer life

Built-up leaves, moss, grime and salts wear down gutters, paint, render and glass. Regular cleaning slows that wear and helps your home hold its value.

Fewer big fixes

Blocked gutters can overflow into fascia, eaves and walls. Catching problems early is far cheaper than repairing the damage later.

What's included

Every visit, done properly

Gutter clean

Gutters and downpipes cleared by hand or vacuum, flushed to confirm the downpipes run clear, and debris removed from site.

Solar panel clean

Panels cleaned with purified water and soft brushes, with no harsh chemicals.

Window clean

Exterior windows washed with purified water and squeegeed streak-free.Glass, frames and sills are wiped down and cobwebs cleared from the surrounds, so the whole window looks clean, not just the glass.

The fine print

Full plan terms

Short and plain. Tap a heading to read it.

Term and renewal

The plan has a minimum term of 3 years from your start date. We contact you at least 30 days before it ends. It renews only if you confirm, so nothing continues automatically.

Pricing and payment

Your price is shown on your quote and includes your plan discount. All prices are plus GST. Your price is locked in: the per-visit prices in your quote will not increase during your plan term, or on renewal while your plan stays active, even if our standard prices go up.

We invoice you just after each clean, due in 14 days. Quarterly or annual upfront payment is available by agreement. If a payment is more than 14 days overdue, we may pause visits until your account is up to date.

Extra work outside the plan, such as extra gutter length or a second storey, is quoted and agreed with you before we do it.

Booking, access and weather

Booking: we send proposed dates by text or email at least 14 days before each visit. Tell us within 3 days if a date doesn't suit and we'll offer another within 14 days.

Access: please make sure we can reach gutters, panels and taps on the day, and that someone can let us in if a gate is locked. Tenanted properties need the tenant's contact details.

Weather: we don't work on roofs or ladders in rain, high wind or when surfaces are unsafe. A weather delay is rescheduled at no cost to you.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time with 14 days' written notice. If you cancel before your plan term ends, you repay the discount you have received up to the cancellation date, which is the difference between our standard prices and your plan prices for the visits completed. We refund the value of any prepaid visits not yet done, less the amount you owe.

You can move a visit once at no cost with 3 days' notice. Later changes may be charged if we have already travelled or prepared.

Our guarantee and your rights

If something we cleaned isn't up to standard, tell us within 7 days and we'll return and fix it at no charge.

Moss and lichen treatments continue working for several weeks after a visit. Heavily stained or damaged surfaces may not return to new condition, and we'll tell you before we start if we expect this.

We take care of your property, and we repair or pay for damage we cause. Nothing in this agreement limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

We use your details only to deliver the plan and send invoices and reminders. Any change to this agreement must be agreed in writing by both parties.
